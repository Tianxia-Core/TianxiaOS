1. Business Overview

RASA-X-MACHINA is an agentic AI education, certification, and sovereignty infrastructure company operating on Base L2. We provide:
  • AI Education Subscriptions — Monthly/annual SaaS plans for AI certification, interview practice, and operator training
  • Sovereign Training Platform — Self-sovereign AI training orchestration (RGU swarm: 54 templates, 207 open allocations)
  • Certification & Credentialing — Verified agentic AI certifications with onchain credential issuance
  • Usage-Based API Credits — Pay-per-inference credits for sovereign AI models
  • Seat Licenses — Enterprise seat-based licenses for our orchestration platform
We sell hosted subscriptions, usage-based API credits, and seat licenses — paid via Stripe Checkout with a Stripe Connect subscription engine underneath.

9. Why Stripe Connect

We require Stripe Connect (not Stripe Payments alone) because: 1. Platform Economics — We collect platform fees on sub-transactions 2. Sub-Account Model — Each training run / certification cohort can have its own sub-account 3. Automatic Payouts — Stripe handles tax withholding and payout routing 4. Connect-Compliant — Our business model is a platform connecting educators, operators, and builders 5. Stripe Tax — Automatic sales tax calculation and remittance for SaaS
